Save

Grow your savings with member-owned banking

An informational look at the savings options a member-owned credit union like AMOCO FCU typically offers, from everyday share accounts to long-term certificates.

Ways to save

Credit unions return value to members through competitive dividends and low fees.

Share Savings

The foundational membership account. A small minimum balance establishes your ownership share in the credit union.

Money Market

Tiered dividend accounts that reward higher balances with stronger rates while keeping funds accessible.

Share Certificates

Fixed-term certificates (CDs) that lock in a rate for terms ranging from a few months to several years.

Youth & Minor Savings

Accounts designed to help children and teens build lifelong saving habits early.

Holiday & Vacation Clubs

Goal-based club accounts that set aside funds automatically for seasonal spending.

IRA Savings

Tax-advantaged retirement savings options including Traditional and Roth structures.

Why members choose to save here

  • Not-for-profit structure means earnings are returned to members as better rates and lower fees.
  • Federally insured share accounts through the NCUA up to applicable limits.
  • Automatic transfers make consistent saving effortless.
  • Local, community-focused service across the Greater Houston and Galveston County area.
