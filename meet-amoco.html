Meet AMOCO

Get to know AMOCO Federal Credit Union

A member-owned, not-for-profit financial cooperative serving the Texas Gulf Coast since 1937. This page provides independent, informational background.

A community-rooted cooperative

AMOCO Federal Credit Union was founded in 1937 and has grown into one of the larger credit unions serving Galveston County and the Greater Houston area.

As a not-for-profit cooperative, it is owned by its members. Earnings are returned to members in the form of competitive rates, lower fees, and expanded services rather than paid out to outside shareholders.

What a credit union stands for

Member-Owned

Every member is a part-owner with a voice in how the cooperative is run.

Not-for-Profit

Profits are reinvested to benefit members and the community.

People Helping People

The guiding philosophy behind the credit union movement.

Locally Focused

Branches and staff rooted in the communities they serve.

Financially Educated

Free tools and guidance to help members build financial confidence.

NCUA Insured

Member deposits are federally insured up to applicable limits.
